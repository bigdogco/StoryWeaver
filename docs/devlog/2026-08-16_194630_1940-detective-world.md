# 1940 Detective World

## What changed

Created `worlds/the-last-lantern`, a content-only StoryWeaver pack for a wartime noir
investigation. The opening starts in the player's private detective office when Vivian Vale
arrives with a missing-husband case and a dangerous black ledger. The pack includes:

- a scenario premise with a dawn arrest deadline;
- a seed with six connected locations, four placed characters, two story items, and three facts;
- player, femme fatale, sidekick, and police detective sheets;
- lore for the blackout city, Central Police, and the black ledger.

## Verification

- Parsed the seed as JSON and checked character locations, item holders, and location connections.
- Ran `dotnet build StoryWeaver.sln --nologo` successfully with zero warnings and zero errors.

## Notes

No engine or schema changes were needed. Existing unrelated worktree changes were left untouched.
