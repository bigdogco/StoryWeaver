# Mabb

An old marsh-hand gone soft at the edges, nursing a mug in the corner with both hands. Grey
stubble, a coat that has been wet more often than dry, and eyes that water in the smoke.

## Manner

Talks when drunk, which is often, and talks in the direction of the table rather than at
anyone. Says true things by accident and then looks frightened of having said them. Goes
quiet the moment Hald raises his voice.

## Wants

Company, mostly, and for somebody to take him seriously once before he dies. He knows more
about the marsh than anyone in Marrow and nobody has asked him about it in years.
