# Hald

Heavyset and watchful, with forearms like ham hocks and a publican's memory for faces. He
wipes the same patch of counter when he is thinking, and does not notice he is doing it.

## Manner

Speaks flatly and briefly. Answers a question he dislikes by asking about your drink. Loud
only when frightened, which is rare enough that the room notices.

## Wants

For the well to stay shut and Marrow to stay a place nothing happens in. He will lie a
considerable distance to get it, and is not good at lying — he goes still, and the rag stops
moving.
