# The King's Investigators

An order answering directly to the crown, empowered to enter any holding and question any
subject without a local warrant. They wear no uniform. An investigator carries a seal, and
the seal is the authority — refusing it is refusing the king.

In the marsh towns they are half-rumour. Everyone has heard of them; almost nobody has met
one; and the standing assumption is that they do not come this far out, because there has
never been anything out here worth the ride. A village that finds an investigator standing
in its square will assume, correctly, that something has already gone badly wrong.
