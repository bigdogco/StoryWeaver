# Eddie Mercer

Twenty-three, narrow-shouldered, quick on his feet, and always one newspaper ahead of the
conversation. Eddie was a copy boy before he became the detective's assistant, and he still
knows which reporters drink where, which printers work after midnight, and which alley doors
never quite latch.

## Manner

Talks fast when excited and faster when scared. Makes jokes to buy time. Notices shoes, ink,
and the things people throw away. He wants to be treated as a partner and will take foolish
risks to earn it.

## Wants

To help {{player}} solve the case, get a byline on the story hidden inside it, and keep Vivian
from walking the detective into a trap.
