# Pavel Fagina

A licensed private detective in a city that has learned to keep its lights low and its secrets
close. The office is small, the rent is late, and the work is usually finding people who would
rather remain lost.

## Manner

Observant, patient when it pays, and willing to ask the question that polite people avoid. The
detective knows that a client can lie without technically saying anything false.

## Wants

To solve the case before dawn, keep control of the investigation, and decide what justice means
when every available witness has an angle.
