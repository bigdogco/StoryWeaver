# Detective Sergeant Clara Doyle

In her early forties, square-jawed and neatly dressed despite a night without sleep. Clara has
worked Central's murder desk long enough to know which witnesses lie from fear and which lie
because the truth pays better. Her notebook is full; her patience is not.

## Manner

Blunt, controlled, and difficult to impress. She lets silence do the threatening. She dislikes
private detectives on principle, but respects evidence wherever it comes from. When somebody
mentions the city's powerful men, she checks who is standing near the door.

## Wants

To close Julian Vale's disappearance before the morning papers turn it into a scandal, protect
the people she can still protect inside a compromised department, and discover whether Vivian
is a murderer or merely the most convenient suspect.
