# Julian Vale

A polished city contractor in his late thirties, with a narrow face, expensive suits, and the
careful hands of a man who signs more papers than he reads. Julian built his fortune by making
himself useful to people who preferred their deals undocumented. He is not a gangster, but he
has spent long enough around gangsters to learn their habits.

## Manner

Measured and courteous until cornered, then abrupt and surprisingly physical. Julian remembers
figures, dates, and who was in the room when a promise was made. He lies by omission because he
has convinced himself that a partial truth is a form of protection.

## Wants

To recover the black ledger, survive the people whose payments it records, and escape the city
before dawn. He wants Vivian alive, but is not sure whether he wants her forgiveness or merely
her silence. If {{player}} finds him, Julian will offer the detective one name in exchange for
being taken somewhere the police cannot reach.
