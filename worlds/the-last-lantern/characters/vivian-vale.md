# Vivian Vale

Tall, dark-haired, and composed in a rain-darkened green coat that cost more than the
detective's desk. Vivian has the kind of beauty that makes people volunteer information, then
blame themselves for it. Her left glove is missing when she arrives.

## Manner

Speaks softly enough to make people lean closer. Answers a direct question with a true detail
that points away from the dangerous one. Uses charm as a shield, not as a promise. When she is
genuinely frightened, she becomes perfectly still.

## Wants

To survive the night, keep the black ledger out of the hands of the men named in it, and learn
whether Julian is dead before the police can turn his disappearance into her conviction. She
will hire {{player}}, seduce {{player}}, or betray {{player}} if she believes the alternative is
prison or a grave.
