# The City Under Blackout

In October 1940 the city goes dark at night under wartime blackout orders. Windows are covered,
street lamps are hooded, and cars move with their lights narrowed to slits. The darkness makes
ordinary errands feel clandestine and gives anyone with a reason to disappear a head start.

People still go to work, hotels still serve expensive drinks, and newspapers still print the
names of the respectable. But after sunset every face is harder to identify and every witness is
less certain of what they saw.
