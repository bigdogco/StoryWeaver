# Central Police

Central Police Station is officially understaffed, overworked, and committed to the law. In
practice, the detective bureau is divided between officers who still believe evidence should
matter and officers who understand that powerful people do not become powerful by waiting for
warrants.

Detective Sergeant Clara Doyle works murder cases. She has enough authority to make a charge
stick and not enough authority to decide which cases the department is allowed to investigate.
