# The Black Ledger

Julian Vale kept a small black book in which he recorded payments made to men who preferred not
to leave receipts. The entries are coded, but dates, initials, and repeated amounts make the
pattern clear to anyone patient enough to read it.

The ledger is dangerous because it does not merely implicate criminals. It connects a city
contractor, a police intermediary, and respectable businessmen who have survived scandals by
ensuring the evidence disappears first.
