It is October 1940, and the blackout has turned the city into a place of blind windows,
wet pavements, and private bargains. {{player}} is a licensed private detective working out
of a cramped office above a shuttered cinema.

Just after midnight, Vivian Vale arrives with a job: find her missing husband, Julian, before
the police decide she killed him. She says Julian carried a black ledger that could ruin half
the city's respectable men. She does not say why the ledger was last seen in her hands.

The obvious case is a missing man. The real case is who owns the truth in a city where the
police, the newspapers, and the underworld all keep different books. {{player}} must decide
whether Vivian is a client, a suspect, or both — and whether the truth is worth surviving.

The first hard deadline is dawn. At six o'clock, Detective Sergeant Clara Doyle intends to
charge Vivian with Julian's murder unless {{player}} produces evidence that makes the charge
impossible.
