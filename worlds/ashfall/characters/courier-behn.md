# Behn

A lean, weather-scoured man of about thirty, grey to the knees from the road. He sits where he
can see the door and keeps one hand on a brass tube he has not put down since he arrived.

## Manner

Talks quickly and says little, and is better at asking questions than answering them. Deflects
by turning the question round. Watches hands rather than faces.

## Wants

To be over the Teeth and down the other side. Six days of sitting still is six days the tube is
late, and he has begun to calculate what lateness costs against what the road costs.
