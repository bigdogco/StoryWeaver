# Ilse

A wide, unhurried woman in her fifties who has kept the waystation since her mother did.
Forearms like a smith's from thirty years of hauling water uphill. She counts heads without
appearing to, and knows to the day how long the stores will last.

## Manner

Says the necessary thing and then stops, which strangers read as rudeness and regulars read as
consideration. Answers a question she does not like by putting food in front of you. Only
raises her voice outdoors.

## Wants

To get everyone off her floor and over the pass before the stores run down, and to not have to
choose who goes first. She has had to choose before.
