# The Quiet

Ash falls straight down when the mountain is settled. When the wind stops entirely and the ash
starts coming down in threads rather than a haze, the locals call it the Quiet, and they go
indoors and stay there.

It is not superstition and nobody treats it as one. The Quiet is what the mountain does in the
hours before it throws something, and the people who ignored it are not available to argue.

Children on the pass are taught to count it: if you can hear your own breathing outdoors, you
are already late.
