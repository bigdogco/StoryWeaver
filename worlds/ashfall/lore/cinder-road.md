# The Cinder Road

The only crossing of the range for ninety miles, cut and re-cut over four centuries as the
mountain buries it. It climbs from the low country through the waystations to the notch called
the Teeth, and down the far side into the plain.

Everyone on this side of the range knows three things about it: that it closes without warning,
that the ash falls heaviest in the last hour before it does, and that the milestones are counted
downward from the Teeth, so a traveller always knows how much worse it can get.

Nobody clears it. The road is not maintained by anyone — it is simply walked until it is
walkable again.
